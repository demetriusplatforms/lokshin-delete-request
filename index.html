Lokshin Account & Data Deletion Request

In compliance with Google Play Store policies, users may request the permanent deletion of their Lokshin account and all associated personal data.

To request deletion without accessing the app, please email privacy@lokshin.app from your registered account email with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".

All user profile data, registered businesses, community posts, comments, and direct messages will be deleted within 30 days of request receipt.
